# Notification decisions

Use these defaults without an interview. Explicit user preferences override them. Infer from current descriptions and explicit choices, not stereotypes or private subject matter.

| Keep | Disable |
|---|---|
| Direct messages, calls, replies, mentions, message requests and friend requests unless already off | Likes, saves, repost counts, profile views, popularity milestones |
| Orders, rides, deliveries, pickup instructions, returns, receipts | Coupons, special offers, upsells, seller/partner marketing |
| Bills, payment failures, account/security alerts, fraud warnings | Generic product announcements, surveys, promotional newsletters |
| Task completion/failure, support case updates, appointments, user-created reminders | Streaks, return-to-app prompts, cart nudges, repeated unread-message reminders |
| Severe weather, lightning, daily local forecast, air-quality/allergy information | General news, trending topics, sports/astronomy news, engagement-based recommendations |
| Birthdays, new followers, communication invitations | Contact-joined announcements, suggested accounts, recommended live streams, routine friend-online/profile-update alerts |
| Explicit saved searches, price/back-in-stock alerts, selected creator/post notifications | Unsolicited price-movement hype, generic shopping recommendations, automatically suggested creator/post alerts |

## Subscriptions and intent

“Subscribed” or “following” does **not** prove every notification was deliberately requested. Do not preserve all marketing just because it was enabled. Conversely, newsletter format does not prove unwanted content.

Strong keep signals: the person explicitly wants it; a named custom alert/reminder exists; a specific creator notification bell was selected; a paid editorial subscription is tied to delivery; a saved search has an explicit alert rule; a particular event was accepted.

Disable signals: welcome/onboarding drip, cross-selling, general offers, recommended stories, broad seller marketing, generic platform digests, preselected promotional categories. Disable these through preferences even when called subscriptions. Never cancel a paid product or leave a rewards program.

For unclear newsletters/content subscriptions, use available subject/category evidence and explicit instructions. If still unclear, preserve and report a concise unresolved decision. Do not unsubscribe from an entire publication because one issue was promotional when narrower controls exist.

A followed account's posts can remain in the feed while broad push announcements are disabled. Preserve explicitly selected per-account notifications. For boards, clubs, communities, and events, distinguish direct communication, deliberate event participation, broad broadcasts, and promotion.

## Mixed or misleading labels

- DoorDash **Reminders** was described as cart items and recent orders; disable those nudges while retaining separate Order Updates.
- Uber **teen accounts** under Marketing explicitly described marketing communications. This does not authorize disabling teen-trip/safety alerts.
- Snapchat **Transactional Messages** mixed friend activity, story reminders, and account information; preserve if finer controls are absent.
- AccuWeather **Weather News** had separate top-story, astronomy, sports, and air-quality/allergy categories. Inspect children.
- Financial-app volume may come from requested alerts, executions, or promotions. Preserve account/security/execution alerts; disable only clearly optional categories. Never alter trades, holdings, billing subscriptions, or alert thresholds to reduce noise.

Do not re-enable already-disabled useful alerts unless asked. Do not change tone, badges, previews, volume, or schedules merely to increase the number of changes. A broad switch that may lose useful alerts remains enabled and unresolved; continue to other settings rather than asking mid-run or declaring the whole app clean.
