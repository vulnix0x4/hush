# Discovery and coverage

1. **Recent volume:** iPhone Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity → intended device → Week → Notifications. Usage time and Pickups are different metrics. Expand Show More to the end. Record displayed counts, not bar-length estimates. Check the prior week if readily useful. If tracking is disabled/empty, continue elsewhere rather than enabling it automatically.
2. **Enabled senders:** OS notification settings inventory apps, websites, and mirrored sources. “Mirroring disabled” does not prove phone notifications are off; “Installed on this Mac” is not a phone preference. Do not toggle controls to inventory them.
3. **Installed apps and badges:** Home Screen/App Library, app lists, or OS settings reveal apps outside the sampled week. Label badges as badges, not notification counts. Notification Center may be stale, grouped, sensitive, or partial. Read enough to identify category/source; do not dismiss or mark alerts read incidentally.
4. **Web/connected accounts:** use open tabs and existing connectors. Website and phone accounts may differ; verify a minimal local alias before treating their preferences as shared. Avoid inbox conversations when only locating settings.
5. **History/mail:** where authorized and supported, begin with a focused recent window (for example 30 days), service domains, sender information, subjects, or promotional labels. Extend only to fill meaningful gaps. No unlimited history enumeration, mailbox dumps, keychains/cookie stores, or token extraction. Queue unavailable sources for the end.

Adapt the order to available access. Do not spend most of the run recovering a preferred source while others are usable. Broad scope means more than the top five noisy apps: follow priority cleanup with the remaining installed/enabled/account sources.

## Queue

For each service/account record discovery sources, device/channel, count/period if known, attempted route, outcome, blocker, and next action. Use “primary account” or “work account,” not full private identifiers.

- **resolved:** relevant settings inspected, changes completed or already quiet, no known in-scope category outstanding.
- **preserved:** inspected and deliberately kept, with explanation. Not a synonym for uninspected.
- **partial:** some categories/channels handled, others uninspected or unresolved.
- **blocked:** no further progress without access/user action; say exactly what is needed.
- **unreviewed:** discovered, not inspected; explicitly unfinished.

Do not add counts across mismatched periods, devices, accounts, grouped categories, or metric types. Historical counts cannot quantify today's improvement. Treat volume as baseline evidence, not a measured outcome.

Checkpoint after completed services or meaningful batches. On resumption, verify current state; old coordinates/states are not executable instructions. Respect newer user choices. Reconcile every discovery entry with an outcome before finishing. Say which sources were inaccessible or uninspected; never infer complete coverage from a partial inventory.
